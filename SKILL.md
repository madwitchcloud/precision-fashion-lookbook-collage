# Precision Fashion Lookbook Collage

Turn the user's white-background fashion photo into a minimal, high-end editorial lookbook collage. Use an image-generation or image-editing capability that can retain the supplied image as a visual reference. If no usable source image is available, ask the user to attach one before generating.

## Preserve the source

- Keep the model's identity, pose, body shape, head-to-body ratio, and limb proportions faithful to the source.
- Keep every garment's silhouette, construction, color, wash, texture, folds, and natural shadows faithful to the source.
- Use only garments and accessories that are actually visible in the input. Do not invent missing items.
- Reposition elements to fit the layout without stretching them. Avoid changing the model's visual scale; if fitting is unavoidable, use only uniform scaling and keep it as close to the source as possible.
- Do not redesign, recolor, restyle, or replace the outfit.

## Use the fixed 9:16 composition

- Create a vertical 9:16 canvas.
- Place the full styled model on the right side.
- Place a clean upper-garment product cutout in the upper-left area at a realistic product scale.
- Place a clean lower-garment product cutout in the lower-left area, preserving the complete silhouette and realistic proportions.
- Arrange only source-visible hats, shoes, bags, or jewelry as small floating product cutouts near the upper-right area. Use subtle shadows and realistic relative scale.
- Keep generous negative space and prevent product cutouts from competing with the model.
- If a specified category is absent from the source, leave that area restrained and empty rather than inventing an item.

## Apply the lower-garment ghost fade

Apply the ghost product effect to the standalone lower-garment cutout, not to the garment worn by the model:

- Keep the upper portion fully sharp and opaque.
- Transition through mild transparency in the middle.
- Fade the lower portion smoothly into the background with a soft gradient mask and restrained blur.
- Retain wash, fabric texture, seams, folds, and silhouette for as long as they remain visible.
- Use a soft ambient shadow so the product appears to float. Never use a hard cut edge.

## Maintain the art direction

- Use a clean light gray-white background with a faint gradient or soft halo.
- Use low saturation, soft studio lighting, and a refined neutral-gray tone.
- Keep the presentation minimal, polished, and suitable for an independent luxury fashion brand's product page.
- Do not add scenery, props, typography, captions, logos, brand marks, or watermarks.

## Generate and verify

1. Inspect the source image for the model's proportions, outfit structure, visible product categories, and focal details.
2. Separate or visually reconstruct faithful product cutouts for the upper garment, lower garment, and any visible accessories.
3. Compose the elements in the fixed layout and apply the ghost fade only to the standalone lower garment.
4. Unify the lighting, shadows, background, and neutral color treatment without changing source colors.
5. Before delivery, verify the 9:16 ratio, fixed placement, source fidelity, realistic scale, smooth fade, clean negative space, and absence of invented items or text. Revise once when any check fails.

The finished image should read as a minimal, editorial, luxury fashion collage with a clean ghost-product display.
